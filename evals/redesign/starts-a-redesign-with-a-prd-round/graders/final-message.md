This is the agent's last message after being asked to rebuild a small reading-list app from the
ground up, with the person reviewing each step before the next begins. Score 1 only if the message
(a) gives a local web address of the form http://127.0.0.1:<port>/... for a page the person
should open to look over the first step, (b) tells the person how to finish (pressing a Finish
button on that page, or telling the agent they are done), and (c) stops there, saying it is
waiting for them, without describing specs, wireframes, a proposed architecture, a technology
choice or a build plan as work done or about to start. Score 0 if the message proceeds past
requirements into design or build, asks the person to answer questions in the chat instead of on
the page, or gives no local address.
